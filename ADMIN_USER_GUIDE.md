# AMBADY Admin Management Guide

**Application Address:** [https://toursandtravels-73c62.web.app](https://toursandtravels-73c62.web.app)

---

Welcome to the AMBADY administrative sanctuary. This guide provides step-by-step instructions for managing sacred journeys, pilgrims, and registrations.

## 1. Admin Dashboard
The Dashboard is your control center for overseeing the spiritual community.
- **Total Pilgrims**: View the count of all registered members. Click **View Details** to open the Pilgrim Inventory.
- **Active Journeys**: Monitor your initiated paths. Click **View Details** to manage the inventory.
- **New Registrations**: Track recent participation. Click **View Details** to see the full registration table.
- **Recent Activity**: A live feed of the last 5 pilgrim registrations with total exchange and status.

## 2. Journey Inventory (/admin/tours)
Manage the life cycle of every pilgrimage experience.
- **Initiate New Journey**: Click the button in the header to draft a new experience.
- **Search**: Use the search bar to find journeys by name or sacred code.
- **Actions Menu (... )**:
    - **Send Announcement**: Prepare an email broadcast for a specific journey.
    - **Edit Journey**: Modify itinerary, pricing, dates, or visual identity.
    - **View Live**: Open the public-facing journey page.
    - **Delete**: Permanently remove a journey.

## 3. Pilgrim Inventory (/admin/users)
Oversee all members of the AMBADY community.
- **Search**: Find pilgrims by name, email, or phone number.
- **Identity Verification**: View masked Aadhaar details and member roles.
- **Management**: Use the action menu to view activity, edit profiles, or suspend access.

## 4. Pilgrim Registrations (/admin/registrations)
Track and process all journey participation.
- **Manual Entry**: Register a pilgrim manually (for off-platform bookings). You can create a new profile or select an existing one.
- **Excel Export**: Download the complete registration list.
    - **Pro Tip**: The export includes an "ALL RECIPIENTS (BCC)" column for instant bulk emailing.
- **View Full Details**: Inspect a pilgrim's address, contact, and Aadhaar identity snapshots.

## 5. Broadcasts & Announcements
To notify pilgrims about a new journey:
1. Navigate to **Journey Inventory**.
2. Select **Send Announcement** from the action menu.
3. **Select Recipients**: Filter and choose which eligible pilgrims should receive the news.
4. **Prepare for Gmail**:
    - Click **Copy BCC Emails** and paste into Gmail's BCC field.
    - Click **Copy Branded Template** and paste into Gmail's compose window.
5. **Verify**: Use the "Verify Layout" section to send a test email to your own inbox via the secure gateway.
